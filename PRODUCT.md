# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js 15 (App Router) + React 19 + TypeScript, plain CSS tokens, GSAP + ScrollTrigger, Lenis smooth scroll, Three.js (hero orbs), Font Awesome Free. Content lives in `src/lib/content.ts`.

## Users

- **Business owners and operations leads** (local and overseas) deciding whether Care Guide can run their back office, support, AI/DevOps, web, SEO or creative work.
- **Job seekers in Dhaka** (English and Hindi speakers, BPO/call-centre experience welcome) looking at careers and blog posts.

## Product Purpose

Care Guide (Dhaka, since 2022) is a back office support and digital technology partner. The homepage must make the offer clear in seconds, prove reliability, and drive two actions: **Work together** (contact) and **Apply now** (careers).

## Brand Commitments

- Logo: the lighthouse mark (navy `#1B2A5C`, amber `#E58A1F`) and the supplied glass 3D lighthouse render.
- Palette is derived from the logo. Navy is the night, amber is the beam.
- Real copy and figures only (1k+ monthly tasks, 3+ years, 55+ team, 99% satisfaction, three 99% bars) as published on careguidebd.com.

## Not verified, not published

- Client testimonials (the live site links to a page but shows none on the homepage), pricing, client logos, contact phone/email, office address. Nothing here is invented; the page links to the live site's contact page.

## Product Principles

1. Say what Care Guide does before decorating it.
2. One idea per section, one authored motion per section.
3. Calm and trustworthy first, impressive second.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA targets, `prefers-reduced-motion` disables Lenis, reveals, pins and orb motion. Touch targets at least 44px. Skip link, labelled landmarks, keyboard-reachable menu with Escape to close.
